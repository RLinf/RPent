# Adoption artwork and sources

The README logo wall contains **13 companies and 25 universities**: all 37
organizations in the supplied `RPent_机构分类与Logo索引.xlsx` register, plus
ROBAM. The register's research cutoff is 2026-11-11; the artwork and linked
project evidence were reviewed on 2026-11-12.

These connections differ in scope. Research affiliations identify authors or
teams associated with a project, rather than institution-wide deployments.
Community listings are references to RPent, rather than evidence of using its
code. ROBAM's collaboration or technical exchange was confirmed by the project
maintainer on 2026-11-12; no public deployment claim is made.

## Relationship key

| Code | Meaning / 含义 |
| --- | --- |
| CODE | A related project integrates or adapts RPent code / 关联项目集成或改编代码 |
| RUN | A related study runs or reproduces RPent / 关联研究运行或复现 |
| RESOURCE | A related project reuses resources / 关联项目复用资源 |
| METHOD | A related study builds on the method / 关联研究借鉴方法 |
| LIST | A community resource lists RPent / 社区资源收录 RPent |
| EXCHANGE | Maintainer-confirmed collaboration or technical exchange / 维护者确认的合作或技术交流 |

The table records a representative connection for each organization. It does
not assert that every author affiliation operates the whole project.

## Display order

The sequence is an editorial assessment of scale, public recognition and
research standing, not a published league table or a numeric strength score.
Companies appear above universities, separated by a thin rule.

For established companies, the leading positions take account of business
scale and international or sector visibility, using primary reports:
[NVIDIA fiscal 2026 results](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx),
[Huawei 2025 annual report](https://www.huawei.com/en/annual-report/2025),
[Tencent results](https://www.tencent.com/investors/results/),
[Xiaomi 2025 annual results](https://ir.mi.com/static-files/640b163f-dacb-4612-a32d-45919fd84089),
[ROBAM 2025 annual report](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-29/1225237757.PDF),
and [SenseTime 2025 results](https://sensetime.com/en/news/51170581).

The remaining companies are ordered by public ecosystem visibility and company
maturity, informed by [Pydantic](https://pydantic.dev/),
[Maincode](https://maincode.com/about), [Qingyan](https://qingyantek.com/),
[Zetta](https://github.com/air-embodied-brain/Zetta-Embodiment),
[HexaFuture](https://hexafuture.ai/home/),
[InfRec / Cardinal](https://www.cardinal-agi.com/), and
[BenchFlow](https://www.benchflow.ai/about). Comparable audited financial
figures are unavailable for this group; adjacent placements are editorial.

University placement considers both international reputation and research
standing, using the [QS 2027 overview](https://www.qs.com/insights/qs-world-university-rankings)
and THE's 2027 tables for the [United States](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/student/best-universities/best-universities-united-states),
[China](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/student/best-universities/best-universities-china),
and [United Kingdom](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/student/best-universities/best-universities-uk),
alongside the [THE Asia 2026 table](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/sites/default/files/asia_university_rankings_2026_-_press_release_clean_v2.pdf).
The sequence combines these signals rather than reproducing any single ranking.
Younger joint-venture universities follow the established institutions;
CUHK-Shenzhen and Duke Kunshan are treated as distinct institutions, without
borrowing CUHK Hong Kong's or Duke University's rankings. Xi’an
Jiaotong-Liverpool University and the University of Liverpool are also separate.

## Artwork maintenance

- `adoption.svg` is the editable, self-contained composition. Each organization
  has a named `org-NN` group, original embedded artwork and a proportional
  viewport. There are no external image or font downloads in the SVG.
- `adoption.png` is the 2400-pixel-wide rendering used by both READMEs, following
  [RLinf's Adoption layout](https://github.com/RLinf/RLinf#Adoption): a short
  introduction, one centered logo wall and an invitation to join.
- Original shapes and colors are preserved. White Qingyan, HexaFuture, MSU and
  Zhejiang marks retain dark fields for contrast. Positive variants for Tongji,
  Nanjing and Beihang come from the official pages linked above.
- The Z-Trans artwork comes from the supplied Zetta paper extract. “Zetta” is an
  explicit project label alongside that mark, not a fabricated independent logo.
- Small institution labels are plain identification text.
  The supplied University of Tokyo artwork includes its 150th-anniversary mark.
- To update the wall, replace or reposition the corresponding SVG group,
  update this register and export the entire white canvas to PNG at 2× size.
  Keep both READMEs' counts and alt text aligned. Check the result at README
  display width and on mobile; the full-resolution image remains zoomable.
- Brand marks belong to their respective owners; the repository's code license
  does not grant rights to those trademarks. The NUS and Griffith copies retain
  the third-party provenance recorded in the supplied material.
