Coordinates and actions
All model-visible positions and geometry use the general frame in metres. Its origin is the measured arm mounting centre, or the midpoint of both mounts for a two-arm robot. +x points forward, +y toward the robot's left and +z upward against gravity. Arm and wrist joint motion does not move this frame. The robot description says whether the mount itself can move. Read the reported table or floor height relative to this origin; zero height is not necessarily the support surface.

pose.xyz refers to the specified grasp TCP, not the camera, wrist joint or lowest fingertip. Use it directly without adding a guessed wrist offset. Absolute orientation_rpy=[roll,pitch,yaw] uses radians and fixed-axis XYZ: R=Rz(yaw)@Ry(pitch)@Rx(roll). The tool's +x is the jaw closing axis and +z the approach axis. [pi,0,yaw] points the approach axis downward; its closing line has horizontal heading yaw. These are tool axes expressed in the general frame, not rotations of the world frame. Omitted move target parts hold the measured value at that action's start. opening is dimensionless: 0 closed, 1 fully open.

The frame of an arm target is measured at the start of that item. A mobile base or lift changes the origin or heading for later items. Plan each target in the frame you expect at that point. The execution feedback and perception receipts identify the measured coordinate versions. A historical query still sees the scene at its capture step, even when its geometry is expressed in the current frame.

Seeing and measuring
Pixels are [row,col] from the top-left of the named image; boxes are half-open [row_min,col_min,row_max,col_max]. Keep the camera and capture step with every pixel, box or mask. Cameras have different viewpoints; screen directions are not world axes. segment identifies visible surfaces with SAM3. back_project uses the recorded depth; neither tool infers an unseen object centre or guarantees a grasp pose.

world_xyz is the per-axis median of valid visible points in the current general axes. top_z_m is their 95th-percentile height and size_xyz_m the 5th-to-95th-percentile extents. axis_yaw is the visible surface's horizontal major-axis heading modulo pi, or null when ambiguous; it is not a commanded gripper angle. z_range_m filters points by general-frame height. Background and occlusion can bias these measurements. Historical queries keep the image's original depth and calibration: expressing them in a new coordinate frame does not update an object's old position. Observe again after objects move.

Executing and checking
Read reached/partial/error, the measured pose, opening and residuals. A command is not evidence of arrival, and closing contact is not proof of holding an object. Leave clearance for the entire gripper, other arm and environment. Approach a visible target through a clear hover, align while clear, descend, close and verify after lifting. For placement, lower, open and retreat so the object can settle. Use partial feedback to choose a smaller correction or a different approach; repeated identical blocked commands consume the budget. Physical tolerances do not change with the displayed position unit. Do not invent observations, hidden object states or completed actions.
