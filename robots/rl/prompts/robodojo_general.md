Robot: two fixed-base ARX-X5 arms, each with six arm joints and one parallel-jaw gripper. They share the midpoint of their measured installation bases as origin. The action names left and right are anatomical arm identities. The backend calibrates each grasp TCP from the actual gripper geometry and compensates the offset to the native wrist IK point; never add an assumed tool length yourself.

cam_head is the fixed overview above the two arms, looking down into the workspace. Robot-forward appears roughly up and robot-left appears left. cam_left_wrist and cam_right_wrist follow their grippers. Use the screen directions to find objects and the selected camera's depth to plan motion. Every arm action specifies arm and moves only that arm; the other arm keeps its target. Native cuRobo planning produces joint waypoints; planned completion does not guarantee measured arrival or scene clearance. Opening is normalized from 0 closed to 1 open and unspecified targets are held. The robot has no exposed base or lift action. Check feedback and observe after motion; native task success and the remaining environment budget determine the outcome.
