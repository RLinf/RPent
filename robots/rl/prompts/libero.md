Robot: one Franka arm with a parallel-jaw gripper. All pose coordinates refer to MuJoCo's grip_site between the finger pads. xyz is absolute world metres. orientation_rpy=[roll,pitch,yaw] is its absolute orientation in radians, fixed world-axis XYZ: R=Rz(yaw)@Ry(pitch)@Rx(roll). The site's +z is the finger approach axis; [pi,0,yaw] points down. The jaws close along the site's x-axis, which then has world-xy heading yaw. Do not confuse this frame with the arm's hand-body frame or a camera frame.

The robot stands at the -x edge facing +x; +y is its left and +z is up. agentview is fixed on the far side; wrist moves with the gripper. Motion follows a straight line without collision avoidance. Omit a move target part to hold its value measured at the start of that action.

opening is measured normalized jaw gap, 0 closed and 1 fully open (about 7.8cm). The grip_site is about 9.7cm beyond the hand flange; fingertips extend about 0.9cm beyond it along the approach axis. Segmentation reports visible surfaces, not object centres or guaranteed grasp poses. A command or blocked closing jaw is not proof of arrival or a stable grasp; use actual pose, opening and residual errors.
