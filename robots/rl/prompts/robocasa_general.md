agentview and agentview_right are oblique overviews carried by the robot. Forward appears roughly upper-left in agentview and upper-right in agentview_right. Robot-left appears roughly image-left in both. wrist follows the gripper. Use the directions to find objects, and the tools' depth measurements to plan targets. Call view_env when you need a new picture after the base or lift moves. Arm motion uses the native Cartesian servo without collision avoidance. move_base uses vx/vy in metres/second for forward/left speed, yaw_rate in radians/second counterclockwise, and duration_s in seconds, then stops. Supply all four values explicitly, using zero for stationary axes. move_lift requests a signed height change in metres, positive up. The controls can share a batch with arm actions. Contacts and joint limits may reduce achieved displacement. For example, after planning a 0.1 metre forward translation toward a cup initially at x=0.4 metres, give the following move_to an x target of 0.3 metres. Choose its y, z and orientation for the planned mount pose. The executor uses the actual measured mount for each arm item.
